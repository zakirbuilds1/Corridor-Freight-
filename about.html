Our story

We got tired ofthe runaround, too.

Corridor started in a two-desk office above a Kansas City rail yard. Our founders kept watching shippers get passed between brokers, carriers and ramps, with nobody owning the outcome.

So they built the opposite: one coordinator per account, every mode under one roof, and one rule. The customer hears about a problem from us first, with a fix attached.

Four rules

Printed aboveevery desk.

They are how we hire, train and review every coordinator.

Rule 01

Own the load

If it’s on our board, it’s ours. No pointing at carriers, ramps or weather.

Rule 02

Say it early

Bad news travels first. You hear about a delay before your customer does.

Rule 03

One voice

A named coordinator and a backup who know your docks, hours and people.

Rule 04

Leave it better

Every lane gets a quarterly review for cost, carbon and on-time performance.
