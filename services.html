Service FAQ

Before you book.

How is pricing calculated?
Linehaul (per mile or per pallet) plus fuel and any extras such as liftgate, detention or hazmat. Every quote is all-in and itemized, so there are no surprise lines on the invoice.
Can you move hazardous materials?
Yes. We move most hazmat classes by truck, rail and drayage with certified carriers. Send the SDS and UN number with your quote request.
What happens if freight is damaged?
Note it on the POD and send photos. Your coordinator files and manages the claim with the carrier. Most are settled within 30 days.
Do you offer contract rates?
Yes. Steady lanes can lock in 6- or 12-month contract rates with guaranteed capacity. Spot quotes are always available too.
